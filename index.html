Our Craft

Not commodity.
Not mass-market.

Marksmen Building & Design exists for the work most shops turn away — the hand-carved capital, the gilded console, the cabinet that takes a season to finish properly.

We pair old-world joinery and carving with modern precision, building custom furniture, architectural detail, and interior finish for clients who measure value in generations, not quarters. Every piece is drawn, milled, carved, and finished with the same exacting standard — whether it stands in a single room or anchors an entire estate.

Process

From first sketch to final finish

  1. Consult

    We walk the space, learn how it will be lived in, and define scope, timeline, and budget together.

  2. Design

    Drawings, material specifications, and species selection — refined until every detail is right.

  3. Build

    Milled, joined, and carved in our shop by skilled craftsmen using traditional joinery and carving.

  4. Finish & Install

    Hand-applied finishes and careful installation, followed by a walkthrough and sign-off.
